Business>Cross-Border Payment

Every global payment method, one partnership.

Accept payments from customers worldwide — offline, online, and everywhere in between.

Cross-border payments are transactions between parties in different countries, involving multiple currencies, FX conversion, and international settlement.

ICB provides a unified infrastructure that enables businesses to accept, process, and settle international payments efficiently and compliantly.

Everything your business needs to collect and settle globally

From accepting your first international payment to reconciling settlements across entities — ICB handles the full flow.

Global & local payment acceptance

Accept payments from customers worldwide via the methods they already use — local wallets, cards, and bank transfers.

Multi-currency processing & FX conversion

Process transactions in 35+ currencies with real-time FX conversion — no manual steps, no hidden rates.

Cross-border settlement & reconciliation

Funds settled and reconciled across countries automatically — reducing delays and manual back-office work.

Built-in compliance & transaction monitoring

Every transaction monitored against local regulatory requirements — compliance handled without extra effort from your team.
