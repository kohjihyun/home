Send money across borders, simply and securely
Remittance refers to the transfer of funds from one country to another, typically sent by individuals or businesses to recipients overseas. ICB provides a remittance infrastructure that enables fast, secure, and compliant international money transfers across supported corridors.

More than a transfer — regulated infrastructure

ICB delivers remittance as a regulated money transfer infrastructure, not just a point-to-point transfer.

  • Send money to 150+ countries through optimized, reliable routes. No more guessing which service will work best — we automatically connect you to the fastest and most stable path available.

  • Convert between currencies with real-time, fully transparent FX rates. You’ll always see exactly how much the recipient will receive before you confirm — no hidden costs, no surprises.

  • Easily receive money from others or send payouts worldwide with complete visibility. Track every step in real time and stay in full control from start to finish.

  • Every transfer is automatically protected by advanced compliance monitoring. You get peace of mind knowing your money moves safely and meets global regulatory standards — without any extra effort from you.
